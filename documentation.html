Service scope

Registrations & Documentation

Axel Nikea helps businesses prepare, compile and coordinate documentation required for compliance, banking, funding, projects, investors and day-to-day business operations.

  • Business registrations and licences: LLP / company incorporation coordination, PAN / TAN, GST, Udyam / MSME, Professional Tax, Shops & Establishments, FSSAI, IEC, LUT, DSC and trademark application support
  • Tax and statutory documentation: GST / TDS working papers, reconciliations, tax computation workings, advance-tax schedules and notice-response documentation
  • Financial statements and certificate support: financial statement schedules, cash-flow statements, net-worth working papers, turnover and fund-utilisation support
  • Bank, loan and funding documentation: CMA data, loan proposal files, working-capital assessment, CC / OD renewal, debt schedules and lender information packs
  • Project and investor documentation: DPR, feasibility report, sources-and-uses statement, financial models, investor memorandum, data-room index and due-diligence checklists
  • Corporate and management documentation: MCA / LLP filing data packs, annual compliance calendars, budgets, forecasts, SOPs, approval matrices and internal-control checklists
  • HR and payroll documentation: payroll registers, salary workings, reimbursements, full-and-final settlement workings and payroll reconciliations

How we support your business

Axel Nikea can prepare and coordinate underlying financial information and documentation. Where an authorised professional signature, certificate, audit, valuation or legal opinion is required, the work is handled through the appropriate authorised professional.

Engagement scope, deliverables, timelines and fees are confirmed after understanding the business, information available and specific requirement.
