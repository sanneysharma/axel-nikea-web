Service scope

Accounting & Compliance

Axel Nikea provides structured finance operations, reconciliations, monthly closing and compliance working-paper support so management can focus on running the business.

  • Bookkeeping and day-to-day accounting
  • Tally Prime setup, cleanup and support
  • Bank and credit-card reconciliation
  • Customer and vendor reconciliation
  • Accounts receivable and payable management
  • Ageing reports and collection tracking
  • Month-end closing and ledger scrutiny
  • Management P&L and balance-sheet review
  • GST return data preparation and reconciliations
  • GSTR-2B / ITC reconciliation support
  • TDS calculations, payment workings and return-preparation support
  • TRACES reconciliation and Form 16 / 16A data support
  • Payroll processing and salary accounting
  • Compliance calendars and finance checklists
  • Accounting SOPs and finance-process documentation
